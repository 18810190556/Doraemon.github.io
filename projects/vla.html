Multimodal robot learning · Ongoing research

Geometry-Aware VLA Policy Learning

Qwen2.5-VL-3BAutoVLAIL / BCPPOLoRA

I extend AutoVLA to continuous field operations, integrating task language, four-frame RGB histories and semantic-geometric estimates in a single trajectory policy.

Architecture and action representation

A joint multi-row encoder represents up to five crop rows, including orientation, spacing, topology, observation support and missing-row estimates. Semantic SLAM, task conditions and strictly past control history enter the multimodal network. Ten trajectory queries extract hidden representations from Qwen2.5-VL-3B. The action head predicts travel distance and curvature, followed by kinematic rollout into ten local poses over five seconds. A learned alignment gate modulates geometric guidance across straight travel, headland turns and row re-entry.

Expert demonstrations and imitation learning

A programmatic planning-and-control expert generates demonstrations in simulation using vehicle geometry and field constraints. Six successful operations yielded 3,492 samples. Trajectory-level behavior cloning uses the expert’s actually executed future motion rather than directly cloning speed or steering commands. Position, heading, distance, alignment and geometric auxiliary losses train the LoRA adapters, structured encoders and action head.

Reinforcement learning and closed-loop execution

I incorporate PPO fine-tuning with expert BC regularization into the IL-initialized policy. The RL implementation uses a stochastic trajectory-parameter policy, task rewards and a training-only critic, updating the trajectory output MLP while keeping the backbone and geometric feedback modules frozen. The controller executes in a receding-horizon loop, with policy queries approximately every 0.2 seconds. RL performance evaluation is ongoing.
